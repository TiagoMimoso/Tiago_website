Poland - Gdansk

As a small weekend gateway, Gdansk mixes the history with the modernity. Impressive vibes and recommended for short.

Italy - Venice, Florence and Cinque Terre

During covid time, I had the opportunity to visit amazing places. Cinque Terre is a place to enjoy and connect with nature and experience what the sun can give you the best with a conbination of a see close by. Florence is a city that you can explore and immerse yourself for days in all the history and being a kid asking why all the time. About Venice, I have little to add to the common description. It is a city that you can get lost and still see places new at every turn.

France - Paris

Short weekend trip to see the major landmarks. From the Eiffer Tower to the Louvre, it is a city of glamour and history.

India - New Delhi, Agra and Rajasthan

Being a tourist and exotic there, experience the challenge of language, food and different principles. Open the horizons to new ways of seeing the world and how insignificant our problems can be. Still more to explore.

New Delhi is a new city in the sence that it offers a unique blend of traditional and modern culture. It was the old city but the new foundations for the modern metropolis.

Agra is the city of the Taj Mahal, one of the most iconic monuments in the world. It is an imponent building that show once again the smalleness of the human being but the clearerness if they work together.

Rajasthan is a region in northern India known for its rich history, vibrant culture, and stunning architecture. It has several famous cities such as Udaipur, Johdpur and Jaisalmer. Johdpur with the caracteristic blue houses contrast and reflect the sun, full of history. I learned that although distance in the old times was limiting but you can connect and that's the beauty of humam mind when you can adsorb so much.
